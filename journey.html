Our Journey

Destinations & Itinerary

A summer dedicated to learning, reflection, and growth. From Cairo to Bosnia, we hope to strengthen our knowledge, build sisterhood, and seek beneficial knowledge.

♡  Support Our Journey

First Destination

Cairo, Egypt 🇪🇬

Summer 2027

Our journey will begin in Nasr City, Cairo, where we plan to spend approximately two months studying Arabic, the Qur’an, and beneficial Islamic knowledge.

We intend to study at Nile Center while dedicating our time to structured learning, strengthening our Arabic language skills, and developing a stronger foundation in Islamic sciences.

Location: Nasr City, Cairo, Egypt

Institution: Nile Center

Duration: Approximately two months

Period: Summer 2027

Our Educational Goals

What We Plan to Study

We hope to deepen our understanding of Islam through structured learning in Arabic, the Qur’an, and foundational Islamic sciences.

Arabic Language

Reading, writing, vocabulary, grammar, comprehension, and conversation.

Qur’an

Recitation, tajwīd, memorization, revision, and understanding.

Islamic Sciences

Foundational studies in aqidah, fiqh, tafsir, and hadith.

✧

Personal Development

Building discipline, strengthening character, and applying beneficial knowledge in daily life.

Second Destination

Bosnia 🇧🇦

Summer 2027

Following our studies in Egypt, we hope to travel to Bosnia and Herzegovina to attend a Muslimah Qur’an retreat organized by Markaz Salih Ibn Suleiman.

The retreat will provide an opportunity to conclude our time abroad in an environment centered on the Qur’an, reflection, sisterhood, and beneficial knowledge.

Destination: Bosnia and Herzegovina

Program: Muslimah Qur’an Retreat

Organizer: Markaz Salih Ibn Suleiman

Period: Summer 2027

The Retreat
