Our Journey · Summer 2027

Seeking Knowledge, Sharing Its Benefit

Our inaugural cohort plans to study Arabic, Qur’an, and Islamic sciences abroad, with the hope of bringing lasting benefit to our families and communities.

Two Destinations, One Purpose

Cairo, Egypt 🇪🇬

Approximately two months · Nile Center, Nasr City

We plan to begin in Cairo, dedicating our days to Arabic language study, Qur’an recitation, and foundational Islamic learning. Our focus is to strengthen our understanding and build habits of learning that continue beyond this journey.

Bosnia and Herzegovina 🇧🇦

Following our studies in Cairo

We hope to attend a Muslimah Qur’an retreat organized by Bosnia Institution—a time for Qur’an, reflection, and learning alongside other Muslim women.
